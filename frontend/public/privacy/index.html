Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ExpenseBeam collects, uses, shares and protects your information when you use our mobile application and our website at expensebeam.com (together, the “Service”). By using the Service you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information You Provide

Account information. When you register we collect your email address and a password. Passwords are stored only in hashed form and are never readable by us.

Financial information. ExpenseBeam is an expense tracker, so the core of the Service is information you choose to record, including expenses, income, account balances, budgets, savings goals, recurring items, categories and any notes or descriptions you add.

We do not connect to your bank and we do not import transactions from financial institutions. Every entry exists because you, or a feature you triggered, created it.

2. Voice Input

The mobile app can record an expense by voice. This requires microphone permission, which you grant explicitly and can revoke at any time in your device settings.

Speech is converted to text by your device’s own speech recognition service, which on Android is provided by Google and is governed by Google’s privacy policy. ExpenseBeam receives only the resulting text. We do not record, upload or store raw audio.

3. AI Processing

Features that interpret plain-language input — parsing a typed or spoken expense, and the in-app assistant — work by sending the text you entered to Anthropic, which returns structured results.

The text you submit to these features, which may include merchant names, amounts and any descriptions you wrote, is transmitted to Anthropic for processing. Anthropic acts as a processor for this purpose and handles the data under its own terms and privacy policy.

If you prefer that this information not be processed by a third party, do not use the AI parsing or assistant features; you can add and edit every record manually instead.

4. Advertising

The free tier of the mobile app displays banner advertising supplied by Google AdMob.

To serve ads, AdMob collects and processes device information including your advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID), coarse device and network characteristics, and ad interaction events. This information is collected by Google directly through the AdMob SDK and is used and shared in accordance with Google’s privacy policy.

You have control over this:

  • On Android, open Settings → Google → Ads to reset or delete your advertising ID, or to opt out of personalised advertising.
  • Subscribing to ExpenseBeam Pro removes advertising from the app entirely, and no ad requests are made.

We do not share your account details or your recorded financial data with advertisers.

5. Subscriptions and Payments

ExpenseBeam Pro is sold as a subscription through Google Play Billing. Payment is handled entirely by Google.

We never receive or store your card number, bank details or billing address. We receive only confirmation of the purchase and its status, which we use to activate and maintain your subscription.

6. Technical Information

When you use the Service our servers process standard technical information such as IP address, request timestamps, device and application version, and error diagnostics. This is used to operate the Service, investigate faults, and protect against abuse.

7. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the Service: authenticating you, and storing and displaying the records you create.
  • To deliver features you invoke, including AI parsing, reports and planning tools.
  • To operate subscriptions and, on the free tier, to display advertising.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse and diagnose technical problems.
  • To respond to you when you contact us for support.

We do not use your recorded financial data to build advertising profiles.

8. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We share information only with the service providers necessary to operate ExpenseBeam:

  • Anthropic — text you submit to AI parsing and assistant features.
  • Google AdMob — advertising identifier and ad delivery data, for users on the free tier.
  • Google Play — purchase and subscription status.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers — who store and serve the Service on our behalf.

We may also disclose information where required by law, or to protect our rights, safety, or the integrity of the Service.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your account and the records you create for as long as your account remains active, so that your history stays available to you.

You can delete individual records at any time from within the app. To delete your entire account and the data associated with it, contact us at the address below and we will action the request. Deletion is permanent and your records cannot be recovered afterwards.

Some information may be retained for a limited period where necessary to comply with legal obligations or to resolve disputes.

10. Security

Traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS, authentication uses signed tokens, and passwords are stored hashed.

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children’s Privacy

ExpenseBeam is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to the Service, our practices, or legal requirements. Changes are indicated by updating the Last Updated date at the top of this page.
